Keyword Painter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03.

Who we are

Keyword Painter is published by Pierre Chevallier, sole trader registered in France, contact: pro.pierre.chevallier@gmail.com.

What the extension does

It highlights keywords you type, in colors you choose, on the page you are viewing, and shows how many times each keyword appears. It runs only when you click the extension (or, in Pro, on sites where you chose "Always on this site").

Data we collect

None. Your keywords and settings are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage); with Pro and Chrome sync enabled, Chrome may synchronize them across your own devices under Google's privacy policy. The extension reads the text of the page only to find your keywords, inside your browser. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

License verification (Pro only)

If you activate a Pro license, the key you enter is sent to Gumroad (Gumroad, Inc.) to verify the purchase, at activation and then about once a week. Gumroad's privacy policy applies: gumroad.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • activeTab, scripting — highlight the page you clicked on, only when you ask.
  • storage — save your keyword lists and settings.
  • alarms — schedule the weekly license check.
  • Optional access to specific sites — only when you enable "Always on this site" (Pro), and only for that site.
  • Optional access to api.gumroad.com — only when you activate a key.

Your rights (GDPR)

We hold no personal data about you. Questions: pro.pierre.chevallier@gmail.com.
